## Case-study evidence intake

For each project, record:
1. **Authorization:** project/client display permission, allowed logos and file usage.
2. **Business problem:** target audience, service, offer and desired customer action.
3. **Exact contribution:** what DigiTera designed, researched, built or launched, and what others did.
4. **Original deliverable:** dated screenshot, working preview, published page, video or client-approved document.
5. **Project stage:** concept, research, design preview, final creative, launched campaign or verified result.
6. **Measurement (optional):** dates, baseline, method, units, attribution limitations and client approval.
7. **Approval:** spelling, case-study copy, assets, legal/privacy review and explicit sign-off before publication.

Never represent a research framework as a completed SEO audit, a concept as a built production website, or visual creative as proof of advertising revenue.

## Review checklist before merging PR #1

- On desktop, tablet and narrow mobile: inspect typography, service cards, evidence index, campaign graphics and fixed WhatsApp CTA. Confirm it does not obscure other actions.
- On the homepage, exercise navigation, filter buttons, project dialogs, video playback, service accordions and contact-form validation.
- On the case-study page, verify Qasr preview navigation, UGC ad playback and the public website context link for Apex. External hosts can change or block content.
- Check the three project statuses against the actual approved scope, and remove or relabel anything the project owner cannot substantiate.
- Check contact details, pricing, privacy copy and all external links with the owner.
- Run `node scripts/check-portfolio.mjs` for repeatable source-level checks. This **does not** replace a real browser/mobile QA pass.
- **Do not merge** or configure GitHub Pages to deploy a feature or preview branch until the owner approves it.

No live branch changes are necessary for preview. Case studies intentionally contain no fabricated conversion metrics, testimonials or guaranteed marketing outcomes.
