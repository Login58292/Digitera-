Strategy study · No ad launch claimed

The focus

Understand how limousine and private transport services are introduced online, and identify areas worth investigating further.

Research scope

Website and service-page review, competitor positioning and recommendations for SEO-oriented content and a stronger enquiry journey.

Recommended priorities

  • Clarify service names and intended audiences
  • Research relevant service and location searches
  • Strengthen key enquiry calls to action
  • Benchmark positioning before selecting campaigns

Evidence note: This is a research and recommendations study. It does not claim that DigiTera built the Apex website, ran ads, improved rankings or produced measured revenue. The priorities above are recommendations, not audited performance findings.

A practical marketing review framework

Research questions and proposed next steps—not confirmed defects, site analytics or completed SEO implementation.

  1. Service intentMap airport, corporate and private-car booking questions to clear service-page topics only where applicable.
  2. Local searchEvaluate search intent, geographic relevance, page titles and internal navigation before recommending new pages.
  3. Trust & clarityAssess how visitors can find vehicle details, service boundaries, policies and enquiry options.
  4. MeasurementDefine enquiries as the primary conversion event, establish a baseline if access is supplied and track changes only after an approved launch.
