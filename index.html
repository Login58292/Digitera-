WHAT CLIENTS CAN EXPECT

Clear work.
Clear next steps.

A beautiful website or creative is only the start. Every proposal should connect the work to a specific audience, a real business goal and a deliverable you can review.

01 / DEFINE

Start with the business question.

We clarify the audience, the offer and the action visitors should take, then agree on scope before production begins.

Potential output: brief & priority list
02 / BUILD

Show the work, not just the pitch.

From SEO content structures to video ads and website layouts, review tangible drafts and completed files at the agreed milestones.

Potential output: designs, edits or page plans
03 / REVIEW

Agree on what success means.

Where analytics are available, set a baseline and review meaningful enquiries or engagement. Never substitute assumed results for measured outcomes.

Potential output: handover & measurement plan

Deliverables depend on the selected service and agreed package; results are never guaranteed.
